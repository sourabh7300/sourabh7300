## The flagship three

Each is a standalone product with its own deployment, its own backend and its own users.

| | Product | Live | What it actually is |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🧬 | **AURA** | **[aura-live-81185.web.app](https://aura-live-81185.web.app)** | A holographic AI assistant with a hand-written 3D particle core that reacts to its state. Voice in and out, Hindi / Hinglish / English mirroring, vision, document RAG, long-term memory — and a **thread-aware chat brain** that resolves follow-ups against the current topic instead of guessing. A gated autopilot squad keeps working, shipping and reporting from the server when nobody has her open. |
| 🎨 | **Lucid** | **[lucid-app-preview.web.app](https://lucid-app-preview.web.app)** | A desktop-class photo studio in one HTML file: WebGL tone curves, HSL mixer, clarity, bloom, bokeh, halation, grain, layers, masks, batch export. **48-template library** with live previews on your own photo, category search, and an AI studio that edits from a sentence or an attached photo/video — with the key kept server-side, never in the visitor's browser. |
| 💡 | **IdeaVeda** | **[ideaveda-live-app.web.app](https://ideaveda-live-app.web.app)** | India's startup decision engine: **40 business ideas** with real investment and success numbers, city-fit scores, unit-economics calculators, 14 funding schemes (Mudra / PMEGP / Startup India), stress-test, plan + pitch generator and an AI Guru that answers in Hinglish. |
